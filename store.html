    About

    How does our Shopping portal work?

    We sell from various shopping outlets for our convenience and yours, which allows direct access to convenient payment modalities.When you click on a link to purchase an item, you are routed to the sales point (Shop) that has the most up to date version of that asset.

    You may alsofollow the selected Shopping Links to specific Asset Sites directly by clicking from the list at the right.

    If you have any difficulties with the asset downloaded please send details description of the issue along with you online contact information for our solution to your issue.All online sales are final due to the un-copy-protected nature of the download.

    There is no DRM restrictions etc. on any purchases but they are under copyright. They are provided under the terms of a permissive MIT license for your personal non-commercial use. You may make as many copies for your own use as you wish and 3D print or alter all models as desired, also for your own non-commercial use. Downloaded products may not be resold.

    Thank you for your interest and patronage. I hope you enjoy our creative works
