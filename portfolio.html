Aircraft and Aerospace Objects


My most current interests extend to graphical simulations and models. This portfolio provides a catolog of my artistic and graphical work.

My simulations are particularly centered on implementation of aircraft and scenery for X-Plan which can be downloaded as freeware for the X-Plane.org community. All 3D graphics are suitable as Unreal and similar environment assets and downloads include several file formats directly including fully editable Blender files and Textures. 3D printable objects, many derived from my own X-Plane simulations are displayed here and may be purchased from the shopping area of my Patreon Page. Otherwork to be displayed here will include links when available for purchase as well.

I appreciate the interest you show in my work and your patronage supports my continuing creation of art works. Freeware (i.e. downloadable materials like the X-Plane simulations not explicitely priced) can always be stimulus for donations in support of my frreware work, via "Buy me a Coffee". I am supported as by subscriptions to my Patreon pages where I discuss the various projects I have, take deep dives into specific ones or explore my creative thoughts.
