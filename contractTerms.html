General Letter of Understanding

3D Model Creation and Related Art-work

I create and distribute, in general, open source 3D models for free use under the MIT license. They are intended for non-commercial use. I have increasingly been asked for specific modeled objects. I will model for requested objects on the above basis of whether the project interests me, particularly for avsim related projects like X-Plane objects. This has been and still is mostly a hobby for me. I will however take on a limited number of commissions or special requests for commercial or exclusive use of contracting individuals. The cost of such are based on the time devoted and an hourly (research and modeling) rate of about $15/hr plus 10%. Note: the more complete the specifications provided the less research time I need to devote, and the lower the hours required. Because I can redistribute any objects I produce it defrays my time and costs. However, exclusive use requests for models will be considered at a higher hourly rate as I would be, by definition, unable to recoup the time costs by spreading these out. I work with Blender and Krita and produce output in Blender, obj8 (X-Plane), fbx,and glb formats for 3D, and png and jpg formats for 2D. Animation will be considered but I do not yet do alot of animated models.

Current rates as alluded to above,

Free, open source, non-commercial models: no charge
Commercial use of any models including commissions: $15 per project hour involved plus 10% (This thus accounts for the level of complexity involved.)
Exclusive use commercial models: $25 per hour x project hours required. (20% in advance to begin project).
Delivery is by digital online means. Hard copy delivery (DVD etc.) is subject to media costs, shipping and handling.
