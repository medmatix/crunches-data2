I design and post Air Bases and Aerodromes from WW-II era for X-Plane Flight simulator. These are historical versions of airports which may be existing in a modern version on the Scenery Gateway or defunct airports no longer existing these days. In particular, I concentrate on in active bases from the Bristish Commonwealth Air Training Plan (BCATP) era.
